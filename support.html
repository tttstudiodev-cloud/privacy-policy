Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored?

All your notes are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and we don't have access to your content.

Do I need to create an account?

No. TagChatNotes works entirely offline without any sign-up or account. Just open the app and start writing.

How do I add a tag to a note?

Tap on the tag icon next to the message input and choose your tag./p>

How do I filter by tag?

Tap any hashtag in the chat to instantly filter and see all notes with that tag. Tap again to return to the full chat view.

Can I back up or export my notes?

Yes! Just click on export to generate a .json file. Import at any time!

What happens if I delete the app?

Since notes are stored locally, uninstalling the app will delete your notes. You can export your chat into a .json file to save it!

Is my data shared or tracked?

No. TagChatNotes does not collect analytics, does not track usage, and does not share any data with third parties. See our privacy policy for full details.

I found a bug or have a feature request

Please email us at the address above with as much detail as possible — your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue help us fix things faster.
