# TagChatNotes

Chat with yourself.
Tag with hashtags.

TagChatNotes turns your thoughts into a conversation. Send yourself a message, add a tag like #todo or #diary, and find it again the moment you need it.

What it does

Write like you're texting a friend

Short notes, long notes, half-thoughts. Your notes flow like a chat, in the order you had them.

Organize with hashtags

No folders, no nested menus. Tap a hashtag to filter instantly.

Everything stays on your device

Your notes live on your phone in a local database. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
